My latest workLet's chat

I like finding out why things work, or don't.

I'm Zainab, a UX/UI and product designer in Madrid, originally from Tangier. I design digital products by looking at how people actually behave, what the product needs and what the code allows.

I've worked on education platforms and B2B SaaS, usually products with lots of services, stakeholders and connected workflows. What pulls me in is research, accessibility, information architecture and design systems, and getting evidence into a shape the people building the product can use.

I started with interior design in Morocco, did a master's in interior design at IED Madrid, then studied product design and moved into digital through freelance projects in 2021. I'm now doing a master's in research methodology for behavioural and health sciences, because I want to get better at asking questions and reading what people do. I also took a break from work for health reasons, and I'm back.

Painting, poetry, theatre and violin have been part of my life since I was a kid. These days I also do aikido, yoga and meditation, which still shape how I deal with attention, empathy and learning. I give talks and run workshops on accessibility, emotional regulation and neuroscience, and I play video games. I speak Spanish, French, English, Arabic and Italian, which helps when a product has to work in more than one place.
