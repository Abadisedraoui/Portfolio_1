How I work

I start by understanding the people, content and constraints behind a product. Research, behavioural evidence and conversations with the team help me decide what needs attention and what to test.

I work across research, information architecture, interaction design and implementation. I document why a decision was made, make accessibility part of the design process and stay close to development as the work moves from Figma into the product.
